Monte Carlo Ecosystem Evolution

Plants, herbivores, and predators evolve on a grid. Each agent uses stochastic birth, death, movement, feeding, and mutation rules driven by energy, local crowding, and resources.

Trait color guide
Plants: greener = denser biomass
Herbivores: warmer / larger = faster, farther-seeing, higher reproduction
Predators: red-violet / larger = stronger hunters with longer vision
How to read the simulation
  • Plants grow and seed nearby cells under a carrying-capacity limit.
  • Herbivores seek food, avoid predators, and evolve through small trait mutations.
  • Predators track herbivores, causing predator–prey cycles and possible extinctions.
  • Dense local populations reduce reproduction and increase stochastic mortality.
