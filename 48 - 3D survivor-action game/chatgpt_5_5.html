Luminara:
Swarmfall

Guide Astra, a crystal-sky ranger, through the living moon-vales of Everbloom. Outrun strange species, collect resonance shards, evolve automatic weapons, and survive escalating bosses beneath an aurora sky.

Original one-file 3D survivor-action game. No external assets. Three.js is loaded from a CDN; every model, shader, effect, sound and rule is generated in this file.
Move: WASD / arrows, or touch-drag on mobile.
Dash: Space for a short invulnerable phase burst.
Fight: Weapons auto-target the nearest threats.
Grow: Pick an upgrade whenever the XP bar fills.
