Model notes

This is an engineering-style planning simulator, not a certified PV yield study. It preserves the supplied monthly 3.6 kWp PV totals exactly and distributes them into synthetic hourly daylight curves.
  • Location/time assumptions: Sofia, Europe/Sofia; daylight values come from your table.
  • Battery default: 10 kWh nominal, 90% usable window, 95% charge and discharge efficiency, 3 kW charge/discharge limit.
  • Heat pump/AC load is temperature-driven from the monthly average/high values, then placed across morning, evening and night hours.
  • BGN costs use adjustable import and export prices. Net cost = import cost − export credit.
Offline single-file simulator. Data and settings stay in this browser only.
