Methodology

How the simulator thinks

  • Hourly dispatch of a typical meteorological day for each month, then scaled by days. Battery SoC carries through the year.
  • PV shape is a sine1.4 curve between sunrise and sunset, normalised to the table’s 3.6 kWp monthly yield.
  • Outdoor temperature follows a daily sinusoid from the table’s low (06:00) to high (15:00).
  • 1.2 kW reverse-cycle mini-split: UA ≈ 78 W/K living zone, COPh = 3.1 + 0.065·T, COPc = 3.8 − 0.05·(T−25), 5 kW / 1.2 kW caps.
  • 120 L boiler: 35 L/person/day (capped at 120 L), inlet water 5 + 0.55·max(0, Tavg) °C, tank 55 °C, 1.05 kWh/day standby.
  • Night tariff 22:00–06:00. Smart mode parks boiler + washer under midday sun.
