Model method: each supplied monthly PV total is distributed over every day of that month using a smooth daylight sine curve centered around solar noon. Appliance demand is scheduled hourly, then the battery is dispatched chronologically: PV serves load first, surplus charges the battery, deficits discharge it, and the remainder becomes grid import/export. The 10 kWh battery starts the year at 50% SOC and continues across month boundaries.

This is an energy-planning simulator, not a utility bill or engineering design. Results depend strongly on appliance usage, battery settings, tariff assumptions and the simplified hourly profiles.
