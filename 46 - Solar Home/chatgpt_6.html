Model assumptions & how the numbers work

Solar and dispatch

The supplied PV 3.6 kWp figures are monthly AC generation in kWh for the eight 450 W panels. Their sum is used without any further weather or loss multiplier.

Each month has an identical smooth solar shape on every day, scaled so the 15-minute steps sum exactly to that month's table value. Daylight is centered on 12:00 in a simplified local clock; sunrise and sunset here are not an astronomical forecast.

Solar first meets demand. Surplus charges the battery, then exports. A deficit draws from the battery, then the grid. Charging from the grid and battery export are excluded.

The year begins at the selected battery reserve. No initial usable charge is gifted to the home. Each month inherits the previous month's end charge. Charge and discharge efficiencies each equal the square root of the round-trip efficiency.

Energy check: PV + import + start storage = demand + export + storage losses + end storage.

Appliances and costs

  • Fridge: rated electrical power × duty cycle × 24 hours. Cycling is averaged across the day.
  • Oven: rated power × scheduled duration, daily.
  • 120 L boiler: 120 × turnovers × 4.186 × (55 − 15) / 3600 / 0.95 kWh/day, plus 0.8 kWh/day standing loss. A 2 kW element supplies this energy from the chosen start time.
  • Heat pump / AC: 1.2 kW electrical input. Heating factor = clamp((18 − average °C) / 20, 0, 1); cooling factor = clamp((high °C − 24) / 8, 0, 1). Auto uses the larger factor. This multiplies the selected active hours; it is an editable scheduling estimate, not a building heat-loss calculation.
  • Washer: selected kWh per one-hour cycle, spread across the chosen number of days per week. A synthetic week starts Monday on January 1.

Net energy cost = imported kWh × import price − exported kWh × export credit. Cost reduction compares against the same loads supplied wholly by the grid. There is no net-metering, fixed fee, VAT adjustment, finance, replacement or capital-cost calculation.

This is a deterministic scenario model. It excludes day-to-day clouds, shading, battery ageing, equipment failures and household loads beyond the five appliances. Inputs remain in this tab until it closes; CSV records the current settings and results.
