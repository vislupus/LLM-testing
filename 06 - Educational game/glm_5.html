📜 How to Play

🎯 Objective

Become a Master Alchemist by collecting elements, discovering compounds, and completing quests!

🎲 Gameplay

  • Roll the dice to move around the board
  • Land on Element Tiles to collect elements
  • Answer quiz questions correctly to earn bonus elements
  • Land on Golden Tiles for special rewards
  • Avoid Hazard Tiles that may cost you elements

⚗️ Crafting

  • Combine elements to discover compounds
  • Each compound earns you points
  • Some combinations unlock special achievements!

🏆 Winning

  • Complete all quests to win
  • The first player to reach Master Alchemist status wins!
