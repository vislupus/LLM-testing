Myco Fact

Grow the forest's secret internet

You are a mycorrhizal fungus. Mother Tree shares sugar (carbon) with you. You trade it for water and minerals you find in the soil. Connect all 6 trees to bring the forest into balance.

🌱 How to play
Click glowing soil nodes next to your network. Costs 1 Carbon. Collect resources.
🌳 Symbiosis
Reach a tree, answer ecology quiz. If you have needed nutrients, trade for seed.
💧 Events
Every 5 turns: drought, storm, beetles. Keep water high!
🏆 Win
6 seeds + at least 5 of each resource = healthy forest.

Field Notes

What you're learning

Mycorrhizal networks are real! 90% of land plants trade sugar for nutrients via fungi. This is the Wood Wide Web. Trees share carbon, warn of insects, and nurse seedlings. You're learning nutrient cycles, symbiosis, biodiversity.
