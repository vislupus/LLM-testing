⚛️ Welcome, Alchemist!

You are a cosmic chemist traveling through the Element Spiral. Roll the dice, move along the path, and answer chemistry challenges to collect subatomic particles.

🎯 Goal

Build as many elements as you can! Collect Protons (determines element), Neutrons (stable isotopes), and Electrons (complete shells). Build Hydrogen through Calcium to win!

🎲 How to Play

Click Roll Dice to move. Land on challenge spaces to earn particles. Land on Build spaces to assemble elements. Watch out for Radioactive spaces — they cost you particles!
