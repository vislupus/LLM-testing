⚗️ How to Play

You are an apprentice alchemist journeying through the mystical land of Eternia, where the elements hold ancient power.

  • 🎲 Roll the die to move along the elemental path
  • 💎 Collect elements by answering questions correctly
  • 🧬 Combine elements to create compounds for bonus points
  • ⚡ Special spaces grant bonuses or trigger challenges
  • 🏆 First to 200 points wins the title of Master Alchemist!
