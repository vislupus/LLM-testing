How It Works

This simulation uses a genetic algorithm to evolve walking creatures inspired by Theo Jansen's Strandbeests.

Controls:

  • Click "Start" to begin evolution
  • Adjust parameters to influence evolution
  • Press R to reset
  • Press Space to pause/resume
  • Press B to show best creature

Creatures with higher fitness (walk further) are more likely to reproduce.
