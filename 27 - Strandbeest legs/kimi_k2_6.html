How It Works
Each creature has 2×N legs (left/right), each a 4-bar linkage driven by a rotating crank. The genome encodes:

• 6 link lengths per leg
• 2 pivot positions (hip, crank)
• Phase offset per leg pair
• Crank speed & body height

Fitness = forward distance − 2×|lateral drift| − 5×|body tilt| − penalty for falling.

The GA uses tournament selection, uniform crossover, and Gaussian mutation.
