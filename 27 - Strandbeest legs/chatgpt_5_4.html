Strandbeest Evolution Lab

A simple genetic algorithm evolves walking linkages. Each genome defines body proportions plus two Strandbeest-style leg pairs with segment lengths, body pivots, and gait phase offsets. Fitness rewards forward travel and stability on flat ground.
