How it works:
• Genome 19 floats 0..1 → crankR, fixed pivot (x,y), 8 link lengths, leg spacing, body height, 6 phase offsets.
• Leg solved via circle-circle intersections (Jansen-style 4-bar + triangles). Brute-force 16 branch combos pick closest to prev frame for continuity.
• Fallback: if Jansen fails, use 2-link IK ellipse for robustness so GA always has gradient.
• Fitness: simulate tripod gait, bodyX += -avg(stance foot Vel X). Penalize falling, bodyY jitter, energy.
• Babylon.js rig: body box + 6 legs × 9 cylinders (crank + 8 links) + joint spheres. Cylinders oriented via quaternion from (0,1,0) to link vector.
• GA: tournament selection (k=3), uniform crossover, Gaussian mutation, elitism 2.
