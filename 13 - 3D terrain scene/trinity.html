Terrain Diorama
Realistic Heightmap Terrain
Procedural generation with peak markers
Controls
LMB Drag to rotate
Scroll Zoom in/out
R Reset camera
F Focus on peaks

Scene Features

  • Realistic terrain from heightmap data
  • Terrain acts as top of a display case/pedestal
  • Walls extend downward creating diorama effect
  • Three peak markers with billboard labels
  • Realistic lighting and shadows
  • Fully orbit camera controls

Note: This demo uses procedurally generated heightmap and textures. To use your own Osogovo images, replace the generated textures with your image URLs.
