AETHER MOVEMENT & JUMPING

Use W A S D to traverse the dimensional arenas. Press SPACE to jump across floating platforms. Press SHIFT to DASH / DODGE enemy tracking. Dashing makes you immune to damage for a short period and activates elemental modifiers based on active sigils.

THE COMBAT SYSTEM (100% MAGIC)

Charge and release core elemental power. Press 1, 2, or 3 to swap spells, or fire directly with dedicated hotkeys:

  • Left-Click (Ignis): Instant fireball, or Hold to charge giant exploding magma blasts.
  • Right-Click (Glacio): Ice spears that freeze, or Hold to trigger a protective swirling glacial field.
  • Q Key (Astraea): Homing starbursts, or Hold to fire a piercing celestial beam.
COMBO AND MULTIPLIERS

Landing successive elemental hits scales your Combo Meter. Maintaining Rank S or above increases Spell Charging speed, doubles Mana Regeneration, and adds bright trails to physical spell castings.
