How to weave.

Iridescence / portals R E

Weave reactions. Ember → rime makes Thermocline. Rime → storm makes Brittle Lightning. Rime → arcane makes Glassbreak. Ember → storm makes Plasma Bloom. Void → any other school makes Unravel. Reactions burst onto nearby foes.

Cast three different schools within four seconds to prime Chromatic Cadence: your next charged spell gains 35% damage. Every hit fills Iridescence; press R at 100% for a protective, restorative burst.

Each arena has two encounters. Gather luminous experience, choose gifts, then walk to a portal. The gentle passage heals you; the daring passage brings more enemies and another gift. Jump over expanding ground rings, dash through danger, and use pillars for cover. Sunthread always costs zero mana.

On touchscreens, use the left stick, drag the world to look, and hold Cast or Focus. Desktop mouse and keyboard offer the most precise control.
