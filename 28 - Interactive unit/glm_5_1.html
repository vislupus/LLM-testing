Our Cosmic Neighborhood

Hello, Space Cadet! Have you ever looked up at the night sky and wondered about that bright, glowing ball? That's the Moon! It's Earth's best friend in space. Let's learn how they dance together!

Earth

Our home! A giant ball of rock and water.

The Moon

Earth's satellite. It orbits around us!

The Sun

Our star! It gives light to both Earth and Moon.

Space Simulators

2. Shadow Play (Eclipses)

Drag the Moon left and right. When it gets between the Sun and Earth, it's a Solar Eclipse! When it goes behind Earth, it's a Lunar Eclipse!

Mission Complete!

Great job, Explorer! You learned:

  • The Moon is Earth's satellite.
  • Moon phases are caused by its orbit around Earth.
  • Solar Eclipses happen when the Moon blocks the Sun.
  • Lunar Eclipses happen when Earth blocks the Sun from the Moon.
  • The Sun is huge, Earth is medium, and the Moon is small!
