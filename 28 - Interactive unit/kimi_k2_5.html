Amazing Facts About Earth & Moon

Our Home: Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place we know that has life! It's covered in beautiful blue oceans and green land.

Fun Fact: Earth is about 12,742 km across!

Our Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It has no air, no water, and no life - just rocks and dust with lots of craters!

Fun Fact: The Moon is about 4 times smaller than Earth!

The Sun

The Sun is a giant star that gives us light and heat. It's so big that about 1 million Earths could fit inside it!

Fun Fact: Light from the Sun takes 8 minutes to reach Earth!
