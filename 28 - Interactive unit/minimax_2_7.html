Earth and the Moon

Welcome to an exciting journey through space! Today you will discover amazing facts about our planet Earth and its faithful companion, the Moon. Get ready to become a space explorer!

The Moon

Earth's only natural satellite. It has no air, no water, and we always see the same side!

Planet Earth

Our home! It's the only planet we know of with life. Earth is about 4 times bigger than the Moon.

The Sun

The Sun is our star. It's HUGE - about 100 times bigger than Earth! The Moon reflects sunlight.

Why does the Moon always show the same side to Earth?

A The Moon doesn't rotate
B The Moon rotates at the same speed it orbits Earth
C Earth is too big to see the other side
D The Sun always lights the same part
Great job! The Moon takes the same time to rotate once on its axis as it takes to orbit Earth once. This is called synchronous rotation!

Think and Write

Why can't a solar eclipse happen during a Full Moon? Think about the positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon!

Hint: Think about where each object needs to be for an eclipse to happen!

During a Full Moon, the Moon is on the opposite side of Earth from the Sun. For a solar eclipse, the Moon needs to be between Earth and the Sun!

🌙 The Moon

Earth's only natural satellite. It has no atmosphere, reflects sunlight, and shows the same face to Earth always!

🌍 Planet Earth

Our home planet is about 4 times bigger than the Moon and about 100 times smaller than the Sun!

🌑 Moon Phases

The Moon goes through phases as it orbits Earth. A complete cycle takes about 29.5 days!

🌑 Solar Eclipse

When the Moon passes between Earth and the Sun, blocking the Sun's light. NEVER look at the Sun without protection!

🌕 Lunar Eclipse

When Earth passes between the Sun and the Moon, casting Earth's shadow on the Moon.
