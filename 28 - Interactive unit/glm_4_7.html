Mission Summary

  • The Moon orbits Earth every ~27 days.
  • Phases happen because we see different parts of the Moon lit by the Sun.
  • Solar Eclipses happen when the Moon blocks the Sun.
  • The Moon is much smaller than Earth (about 1/4 the diameter).
