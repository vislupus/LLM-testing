🌟 Fun Facts About the Moon

🛰️

Only Natural Satellite

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It travels around us in space!

🪞

Mirror in the Sky

The Moon does not make its own light. It shines by reflecting sunlight.

🌑

Always One Face

We always see the same side of the Moon because its rotation matches its orbit.

🌗

Changing Shapes

The Moon seems to change shape. These are called phases. They repeat about every 29 days.

🌑🌎

Amazing Eclipses

When Earth, Moon, and Sun line up, we can see lunar or solar eclipses!

🏜️

No Air, No Water

The Moon has no atmosphere and no liquid water. Its surface is dusty and full of craters.

3. Why We See Only One Side

The Moon spins on its axis exactly once for every trip around Earth. This is called tidal locking. Press play and watch the arrow always point at Earth!

Try this: Can you see that the same face of the Moon always looks toward Earth? That's why we never see the "far side" from home!

📝 What We Learned

  • ✦ The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite.
  • ✦ It shines by reflecting sunlight — it does not make its own light.
  • ✦ The Moon has no atmosphere and no liquid water.
  • ✦ It orbits Earth and rotates on its axis in the same amount of time.
  • ✦ Moon phases repeat about every 29 days.
  • ✦ A lunar eclipse happens when Earth's shadow falls on the Moon.
  • ✦ A solar eclipse happens when the Moon blocks the Sun's light.
  • ✦ Never look directly at the Sun — it can hurt your eyes!
