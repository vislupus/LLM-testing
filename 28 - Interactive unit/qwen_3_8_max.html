🚀 Meet the Moon!

Short facts, big wonders. Read, tap and enjoy!

🛰️

Our only satellite

The Moon is the only natural satellite of the Earth. It orbits around our planet.

🪞

A giant mirror

The Moon makes no light of its own. It only reflects the light of the Sun.

📏

A tricky look

The Moon looks almost as big as the Sun — but only because it is much closer to us!

️

A bumpy world

The Moon is airless and lifeless, with no atmosphere. In 1608, Galileo Galilei saw its rough surface full of craters — holes made by impacts over millions of years.

🔄

Two dances at once

The Moon orbits Earth and spins on its axis in the same time. That is why we always see the same side!

🌗

The Moon clock

The Moon's shape keeps changing — its phases. The time between two same phases is 29 days and 12 hours = one lunar month.

🌑 The Moon's bumpy surface

Craters = dents from impacts over millions of years.

🌍 Earth & Moon from space

The Moon is ~4× smaller than Earth. Earth is ~100× smaller than the Sun!

📌 Summary — The Most Important

⭐ Remember!

  • The Moon's phases change because the Moon moves around Earth.
  • A lunar eclipse happens when the Moon enters Earth's shadow (Sun–Earth–Moon).
  • A solar eclipse happens when parts of Earth fall into the Moon's shadow (Sun–Moon–Earth) and the Sun's corona shines.
  • The Moon spins once per orbit → we always see the same side.
  • Lunar month = 29 days and 12 hours.
