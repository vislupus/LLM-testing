Our beautiful planet and its closest friend

The Moon is Earth’s only natural satellite. It doesn’t make its own light — it reflects light from the Sun! It is about 4 times smaller than Earth and 100 times closer than the Sun.
🌕 The Moon has no air or water
🕳️ Lots of craters from space rocks
