Introduction: Our Neighbor in Space

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. Even though it looks big in the sky, it is actually much smaller than Earth (about 4 times smaller in diameter!).

It has no air (atmosphere) and no water. In 1608, the astronomer Galileo Galilei used a telescope to see that the Moon's surface is rough, full of mountains and holes called craters.

Did You Know?

  • The Moon shines because it reflects sunlight.
  • It takes about 29 days for the Moon to go around Earth.
  • We always see the same side of the Moon!

Summary

  • The Moon orbits Earth every ~29 days.
  • Phases change based on how much sunlight hits the Moon.
  • Solar Eclipse: Sun - Moon - Earth.
  • Lunar Eclipse: Sun - Earth - Moon.
  • Warning: Never look directly at the Sun during an eclipse without special glasses!
