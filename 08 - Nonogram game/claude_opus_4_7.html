How to play

  1. Numbers at the top and left count runs of filled cells in that column or row.
  2. Click or drag to fill cells.
  3. Right-click or Shift+click marks a cell with an X — your note that the cell is empty.
  4. Hover a cell to highlight its row and column.
  5. Press Check when you think you've solved it.
