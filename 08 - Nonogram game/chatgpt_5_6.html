Happy Robot Picross

Use the number clues to uncover the hidden 20×20 picture.
20 × 20
Left-click to fill. Right-click to add an X. Drag to mark several cells.

How to play

  1. Each clue describes consecutive filled blocks in that row or column.
  2. Separate clue numbers require at least one empty cell between blocks.
  3. Fill picture cells and mark known empty cells with an X.

Touch controls

Choose a mode, then tap or drag across the puzzle.
