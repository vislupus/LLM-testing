How this dashboard works Self-contained, offline-capable

Data: Loads temperature_Sofia_2809794_1952-01-01 - 2021-11-28.csv via fetch() from same folder. If blocked by CORS (file://), use file picker.

Parsing: DATE, TAVG, TMAX, TMIN in tenths of degrees → °C (÷10). Missing values (-9999 or empty) are ignored.

Aggregates: Yearly mean = mean of daily TAVG; extremes = max TMAX / min TMIN per year; hot = TMAX ≥30°C; cold = TMIN ≤−10°C; monthly means = mean TAVG per calendar month.

Trend: Least-squares regression on annual mean vs year; slope ×10 = °C/decade.

Controls: Change Start/End year — all charts, KPIs and table update instantly.
