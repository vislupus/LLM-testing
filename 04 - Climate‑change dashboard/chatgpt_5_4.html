Sofia Climate Dashboard

Interactive climate explorer for Sofia based on daily observations from a local CSV file. The dashboard converts temperatures from tenths of degrees to °C, computes annual and monthly aggregates, counts very hot and very cold days, and estimates the long-term warming trend.

Notes. This page expects a CSV file with columns DATE, TAVG, TMAX, and TMIN. Temperature values are assumed to be in tenths of °C and are converted in the browser. Missing values are ignored. If TAVG is absent for a day but both TMAX and TMIN are present, the dashboard uses their midpoint as a fallback daily mean.
