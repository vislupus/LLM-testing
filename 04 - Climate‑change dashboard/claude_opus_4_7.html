METHODOLOGY. Temperatures in the source CSV are encoded in tenths of a degree Celsius per NOAA's GHCN-Daily convention; this page divides by ten before aggregating. Yearly and monthly means are computed from daily TAVG where available, otherwise as (TMAX + TMIN) / 2. The warming trend is an ordinary least-squares fit to annual means within the selected range.
