Sofia Climate Dashboard

Browser-based analysis of daily temperature observations for Sofia. The page loads temperature_Sofia_2809794_1952-01-01 - 2021-11-28.csv from the same folder, converts tenths of degrees to °C, and updates all charts when the year range changes.
