How the dashboard calculates each metric

Unit conversionTAVG, TMAX, and TMIN values are divided by 10 because the source stores temperatures in tenths of a degree Celsius.
Annual meanThe arithmetic mean of all valid daily TAVG observations within each calendar year.
ExtremesThe largest valid TMAX and smallest valid TMIN observed in each year and selected range.
Warming estimateAn ordinary least-squares linear trend fitted to yearly mean temperatures, reported in °C per decade.
