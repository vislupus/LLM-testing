The Hollow Tide

The Sun-Forge of Emberhall kept the mist at bay for nine hundred years. Last winter it guttered out, and from the drowned valleys crawled the Hollow Tide — pale, patient, endless. You are the last Warden of the Marches. Raise timber, mine amber, bind villagers to your banner, and hold the line through the nights. When your armies are strong, break the Rift Spires that bleed the mist — and end the Tide at its Hollow Heart.

THE WARDEN'S PRIMER

Close
Days are for growth: villagers gather Amber, Timber and Relic Crystal; you build, upgrade and recruit. Nights bring the Hollow Tide — waves grow every night, and elites lead them from night four. Win by shattering all four Rift Spires, then destroying the exposed Hollow Heart. Or survive 15 nights to see the mist break on its own. Lose if your Keep falls.

Villagers auto-gather from nodes — click a node to assign or pull workers. Build Dwellings for population, Watchtowers and Walls to funnel the enemy, and Mills to boost nearby nodes. Drag-select troops, right-click to move or attack. Use Attack-Move to push into the mist. Group troops with Ctrl+1..9.
