⚔️ How to Play

Goal: Defend your kingdom and destroy all 4 Shadow Citadels.

Controls:
WASD / Arrow Keys — Pan camera
Mouse Wheel — Zoom
Right-Click + Drag — Rotate camera
Left-Click — Select / Place
Drag Left-Click — Box select units
Right-Click — Move / Cancel
A + Right-Click — Attack-Move
Q — Select all military
E — Select idle workers
1-5 — Quick build (House/Mill/Farm/Barracks/Tower)
Esc — Cancel / Deselect
Space — Center on base
H — Toggle help

Tips:
• Build Lumber Mills and Farms early for resources.
• Houses increase your population cap.
• Build Towers near your base for defense.
• Train an army before the first wave!
• Upgrade your economy before expanding military.
