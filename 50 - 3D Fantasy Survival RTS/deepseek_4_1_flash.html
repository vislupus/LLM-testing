EMBERFALL

Realms at War · a stylized fantasy survival RTS

The Ashen Warcamp rises in the east. Gather lumber, gold and crystal, raise a keep, expand your territory, research the old arts and command legions in the ember-lit fields of the Fall. Survival is the strategy.

ECONOMY

Select workers and right-click 🪵 trees, 🪙 gold veins or 💠 crystal shards to harvest. Bring hauls to a friendly building. Farms, Camps and Mines boost nearby yields. Houses raise population.

COMMAND

Left-drag = box select · Left-click = pick · Right-click = move / attack / gather · A attack-move · S stop · H hold · F cycle formation · B build menu · R research · Space centre keep

WAR

Each wave strikes a weaker structure — towers, walls and the Ember Knight hero hold the line. Promote buildings to unlock more units, upgrade the Academy for deeper research, then march east and shatter the Warcamp.

WORLD

✦ Fog of war hides the wilds — scout with towers.
✦ Night emboldens the enemy: prepare before dusk.
✦ Crystal fuels mages and arcane research.
✦ Difficulty and pacing can be restarted at any time.
