THE MARSHAL’S FIELD GUIDE

From a village to a vow.

01 / A living economy

Your eight starting gatherers already work. Recruit more at the Hall. Select workers and right click trees, stone, crystal, berry bushes, or a Sunseed Garden. Workers carry resources to the Hall or a Gatherer Lodge. Short journeys make a rich kingdom.

02 / Build with intention

Press B and choose a structure. Green ground is buildable; red explains what is missing. Houses add population. Gardens sustain recruitment. Warden Yards train your first army. Construction and repairs need workers. Select a building to upgrade it or set its rally point with right click.

03 / Own the crossings

The river has three bridges. Infantry protects ranged troops, Mossriders hunt archers, and Sunbinders punish crowds. Line, square, and wedge formations change how your army arrives. Build walls and bastions to shape the enemy’s approach. Friendly troops pass through Boughgates.

04 / Push back the dark

Scout with the Marshal. An army near an uncontested ancient waylight captures it, gaining territory and crystal. Build your own Waylights to extend the frontier. Enemy courts recruit and seek weak settlements. Their larger night marches grow more dangerous each cycle.

05 / The Marshal’s magic

Q casts Lantern Rally, healing and inspiring allies within 13 paces. E prepares Starfall: click within 20 paces of Lio to damage and stun enemies. Recall a fallen Marshal at the Hall. Research at an Observatory strengthens your whole kingdom.

06 / A dawn without masters

Destroy all three Hollow Courts and defeat the Briar Regent, who arrives with the sixth night. Destroyed courts reduce later waves. Hold the Hall at all costs. A cycle lasts 105 seconds; the first 65 are daylight. Use pause to plan your next move.
