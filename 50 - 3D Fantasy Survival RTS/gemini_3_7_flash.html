👑 Basic RTS Controls
  • Left Click: Select building or unit. Drag to box-select multiple units.
  • Right Click: Move army, attack enemy target, or assign villager to harvest/repair.
  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Pan the camera across the kingdom.
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom camera in and out.
  • Spacebar: Pause/Resume game or center on your Hero / Citadel.
  • Q, W, E, R: Cast your Commander's heroic spells!
☀️ Day & Night Survival Loop

During Daytime (60s), harvest resources, expand your base, construct Watchtowers and Walls, recruit archers and infantry, and upgrade your Citadel. You can also venture into the fog to destroy Void Rifts for legendary loot!

When Night falls, the Abyssal Swarm attacks! Position your army behind walls, garrison towers, and use your Hero spells to survive until the morning sun rises.

🛡️ Unit Counters
  • Infantry / Shieldguard: High armor, absorbs damage and holds chokepoints.
  • Archers / Crossbows: High single-target DPS from behind walls.
  • Pyromancers / Mages: Massive AoE spell splash to incinerate swarms.
  • Knights / Paladins: Fast mobile flankers that trample squishy ranged foes.
  • Catapults & Ballistas: Extreme range siege weapons that pulverize enemy clusters.
