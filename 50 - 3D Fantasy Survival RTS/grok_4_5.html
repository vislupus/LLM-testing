How to Play

Controls

  • Left-click select unit/building · Drag box-select
  • Right-click move / attack / gather
  • WASD / Arrows / Edge-scroll / Middle-mouse pan · Scroll zoom
  • Space pause · 1/2 speed · Esc cancel build
  • F focus selection · H hero · G gather · T/Y attack/defend stance

Economy

Assign Workers to Gold Veins, Timber Groves, and Stone Outcrops. Build Mills and Quarries near nodes. Houses raise population cap. The Bastion generates slow mana.

Military

Recruit from the Barracks, Range, and Sanctum. Use Attack/Defend stances. Formations matter — melee front, ranged behind. Towers and Walls hold chokepoints. Your Hero has a powerful pulse ability (hotkey H then ability button).

Survival

Days bring building time. Nights bring Dusk Legion raids that grow deadlier. Survive until Day 12 and destroy the Enemy Spire, or fall if your Bastion is destroyed.

Fog of War

Unexplored land is hidden. Units and buildings reveal the map. Scout wisely before expanding.
