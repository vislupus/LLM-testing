Luminwood Crown
A stylized miniature realm of banners, brave folk and midnight sieges. Gather, build, research and command real-time battles under fog of war.
Goal
Destroy the Obsidian Gate while keeping your Hearthspire alive.
Pressure
Night raids become giant waves. Elite creatures emerge as days pass.
Control
Drag-select units. RMB move/attack. Build territory with banners.
Readability
Strong silhouettes, color-coded teams, clean UI, and fog-aware minimap.
Realm Settings
Difficulty
Affects wave size, elite rate, and enemy aggression.
Seed (replayable)
Leave blank for a random realm.
Tip: Build a Watchtower early, then expand with Banner Post to secure resources.
How to Play
Selection & Orders
  • LMB click selects; drag to box-select.
  • Shift adds/removes from selection.
  • RMB on ground: move in formation.
  • RMB on enemy: focus attack.
  • Space: pan camera to selection.
Economy
  • Workers auto-assign to Timber/Stone/Aether by sliders.
  • Build Farmstead for population cap and small food income.
  • Build Banner Post to expand territory & vision.
Defense & Survival
  • Nights bring raids and escalating waves.
  • Watchtowers reveal fog and pick off raiders.
  • Palisades block melee and shape battles.
Victory
  • Destroy the enemy Obsidian Gate.
  • Protect your Hearthspire. If it falls, your realm is lost.
