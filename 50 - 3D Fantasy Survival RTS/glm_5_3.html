A Tale of the Last Vale
Choose your burden — the horde grows fiercer each night.
Gather. Drag-select your Villagers and right-click trees, berry bushes, rocks and gold veins. They haul everything back to the Keep.
Build. Press B (or the Build button). Cottages raise population, Farmsteads grow food, the Barracks trains soldiers, the Smithy researches upgrades. New buildings must be placed near existing ones — expand across the vale.
Command. Right-click to move or attack; groups march in formation and fight on their own. Longbows outrange melee — screen them with Swordsworn and Knight-Errants.
Survive the night. Each nightfall the Gnarlfang Horde attacks, and every night is worse. Watchtowers, palisades and a trained army hold the line. Elites and the Warchief come later.
Win. Destroy the Gnarlfang Citadel in the far northeast. Scout first — the land is dark until you walk it.
Camera. WASD/edges pan · Q/E rotate · wheel zoom · H center on Keep · S stop · P pause. Best played with mouse & keyboard.
Everything in this world is generated on the spot — terrain, armies, music and all.
