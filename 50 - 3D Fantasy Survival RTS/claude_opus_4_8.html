How to play
• Left-drag to box-select. Right-click to move / gather / attack / build-assist.
• Send Workers to forests (wood) and crystal veins (gold). Build Houses for population.
• Build a Barracks to recruit soldiers, Towers & Walls to defend, a Workshop to research.
• Survive 10 nights, or raze every enemy Warcamp, to win. If your Keep falls, you lose.
• Keys: A attack-move · S stop · H hold · Q hero ability · Space pause · 1-9 control groups (Ctrl to set)
