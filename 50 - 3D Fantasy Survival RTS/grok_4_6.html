HOW TO HOLD THE VALE

  • Left drag selects a host. Right click is a smart command: move, strike, or gather.
  • Valehands harvest Golden Groves, Marble, and Prism Shards. Return bounty to the Sunkeep or drop-halls.
  • Build only on claimed ground near your halls. Watchfires and towers push the border into the wild.
  • Days are for raising walls, research and banners. Nights birth the Bloom — first as skirmishes, later as tides.
  • Hold the river fords. Train mixed hosts. Form lines. Call Caelia’s Solar Pulse when the dark thickens.
  • Victory: destroy the Nightbloom Heart, or endure 12 nights. Defeat: the Sunkeep falls.
  • Keys: WASD pan, Q/E rotate, wheel zoom, Space pause, F idle hands, H hero, Z solar pulse, Esc cancel, Ctrl+1–4 control groups.
