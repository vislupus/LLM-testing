Real-Time Ray Tracing in Babylon.js

This simulation features an analytical, multi-bounce GPU ray tracer embedded inside the Babylon.js rendering pipeline. Unlike conventional rasterized environment mapping or screen-space reflections (SSR), this engine performs true ray-geometry intersection testing for every pixel.

Core Features Implemented:

  • Mutual Recursive Inter-Reflections: Rays bouncing off Sphere 1 hit Sphere 2, which bounce back to Sphere 1, reflecting the surrounding street view recursively up to 6 bounces.
  • Microfacet GGX Roughness: Reflection rays are perturbated based on user-defined roughness, allowing transitions from flawless mirror finish to brushed metal and satin textures.
  • Conductor Fresnel Equations: Schlick approximation reproduces exact metallic reflectance characteristics for Chrome, 24K Gold, Copper, Silver, Titanium, and Obsidian.
  • Photorealistic 3D Street: Featuring asphalt textures, road markings, pedestrian zebra crossing, raised sidewalk curbs, 3D lampposts, and dynamic wet puddles.
  • Ray-Traced Soft Penumbra Shadows: Analytical sphere shadow rays compute contact shadows that soften as the spheres hover higher above the pavement.
  • Suspended Rigging: Fine vertical steel cables suspend each heavy metallic sphere above the city street.

Navigation & Controls:

• Left Mouse / Touch Drag: Orbit camera around the spheres.
• Right Mouse / Two-Finger Drag: Pan camera position.
• Scroll Wheel / Pinch: Zoom in and out.
• Click Spheres: Click directly on either sphere to select and tweak its material parameters.
