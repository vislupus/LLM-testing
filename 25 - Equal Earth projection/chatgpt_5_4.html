Equal Earth World Map

A single-file SVG world map with the Equal Earth projection, forward and inverse coordinate conversion, live marker plotting, and mathematically correct pan/zoom.

Click the map to invert from screen coordinates back to longitude/latitude. Shift-click also drops a marker at the clicked geographic position.

Drag to pan. Use the mouse wheel to zoom around the cursor. Double-click resets the view.
