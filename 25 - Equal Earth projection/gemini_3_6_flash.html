Equal Earth Projection Mathematics

The Equal Earth map projection is an equal-area pseudocylindrical projection created by Bojan Šavrič, Bernhard Jenny, and Tom Patterson in 2018. It retains exact area proportions across the entire globe while maintaining pleasing curved meridians.

Parametric Angle θ:
sin θ = (√3 / 2) · sin φ
Polynomial Denominator (denom):
denom = A₁ + 3 A₂ θ² + 7 A₃ θ⁶ + 9 A₄ θ⁸
Forward Coordinates (x, y):
x = [ 2√3 · (λ - λ₀) · cos θ ] / [ 3 · denom ]
y = θ · ( A₁ + A₂ θ² + A₃ θ⁶ + A₄ θ⁸ )
Polynomial Constants (Šavrič et al. 2018):
A₁ = 1.340264
A₂ = -0.081106
A₃ = 0.000893
A₄ = 0.003796

Inverse Projection (Screen x, y → φ, λ):

Inverse conversion computes θ from projected y using Newton-Raphson numerical iteration: θn+1 = θn - [ y(θn) - y ] / y'(θn), where the derivative y'(θ) = denom. Convergence occurs in 3–5 steps to 10-12 machine precision.
