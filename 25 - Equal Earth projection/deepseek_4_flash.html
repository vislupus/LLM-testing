    Equal Earth projection

    Equal-area pseudocylindrical projection (Šavrič, Patterson & Jenny, 2018). sin θ = (√3/2)·sin φ  ·  A₁=1.340264, A₂=−0.081106, A₃=0.000893, A₄=0.003796

    x = 2√3·λ·cos θ / (3·(9A₄θ⁸ + 7A₃θ⁶ + 3A₂θ² + A₁))
    y = A₄θ⁹ + A₃θ⁷ + A₂θ³ + A₁θ
    inverse: solve θ-polynomial (Newton), then
    λ = 3qx/(2√3·cos θ)  ·  φ = asin(2·sin θ/√3)
    • · equal-area: Jacobian = cos φ exactly
    • · straight parallels, curved meridians
    • · world aspect ≈ 2.05 : 1
    • · zoom/pan = affine transform of projected plane → exact at any scale
