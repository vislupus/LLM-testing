Equal-Area Projection

Equal Earth

A pseudocylindrical projection preserving the true relative size of continents. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click anywhere to invert screen pixels back to latitude and longitude.

▶ Projection formulas
Forward (φ, λ → x, y)
θ = arcsin(√3/2 · sin φ)
x = (2√3/3) · λ · cos θ
y = (√3/3) · (sin φ · sin θ + 2 − 2 cos θ) / cos θ
Inverse (x, y → φ, λ)
Solve φ by bisection (y is monotonic in φ), then:
λ = x / ((2√3/3) · cos θ)
