The Equal Earth projection is a pseudocylindrical, equal-area projection developed in 2018. It perfectly preserves relative land sizes while maintaining highly recognizable continental shapes.

Draws circles of equal area on the sphere. On our Equal Earth map, they flatten near the poles but their total surface area remains perfectly constant, illustrating the projection's math.

Radius: 6.0°
1. Parametric latitude θ: θ = arcsin( (√3 / 2) * sin(φ) )
2. Forward Projection (x, y): x = R * λ * cos(θ) / [ (√3 / 2) * D(θ) ]
y = R * θ * D(θ)
3. Where D(θ) is the polynomial: D(θ) = A₁ + A₂*θ² + A₃*θ⁶ + A₄*θ⁸
A₁ = 1.340264, A₂ = -0.081106
A₃ = 0.000893, A₄ = 0.003796
4. Inverse Newton-Raphson Iteration: Solves θ from y by iteratively correcting:
Δθ = (θ*D(θ) - y/R) / [ A₁ + 3*A₂*θ² + θ⁶*(7*A₃ + 9*A₄*θ²) ]
Then λ and φ are solved directly!
