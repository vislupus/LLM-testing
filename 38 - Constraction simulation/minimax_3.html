Structure Lab is an interactive physics simulator.

Build structures out of beams, secure them with supports, and apply loads. Then tune environmental forces and watch how your design holds up.

Tools
• Select — click to inspect, drag nodes
• Beam — connect two nodes
• Support — fixed/roller ground anchor
• Load — apply point force to a node
• Delete — remove elements

Tip: hold Space to pan, scroll to zoom.
