StrucLab User Guide & Quick Controls

How to design, simulate, and stress-test physical structures

Construction Tools

  • B Beam: Click & drag between grid points to create structural members.
  • C Cable: Tension-only wires (snaps if slack, ideal for suspension).
  • P Piston: Dynamic expanding/contracting actuator.
  • S Support: Click a node to cycle or set Ground Anchor supports.
  • W Weight: Click any node to add heavy concentrated cargo.
  • X Cut / Eraser: Click members to delete or test severance.

Physics & Navigation

  • F Pull Force: Click and drag any node with mouse to exert live tensile/compressive loads!
  • V Inspect: Hover/click elements to see real-time Stress, Safety Factor, and Yield status.
  • Pan Camera: Right-click + drag, or Middle-click + drag, or hold Space + Left drag.
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel up/down anywhere on canvas.

Environmental Stress Conditions

Use the right-hand sidebar to trigger Hurricane Crosswinds, Heavy Rain Mass Accumulation, Seismic Earthquakes with adjustable resonance frequencies, and Thermal Expansion. Watch how stress redistributes across trusses and observe weak points before structural failure occurs!

Failure Criteria

• Tension Rupture: Occurs when axial stress exceeds the material's Ultimate Tensile Strength.
• Euler Buckling: Slender compressive struts collapse under critical buckling load: \(P_{cr} = \pi^2 E I / L^2\).
• Factor of Safety (FoS): Values below 1.0 indicate active failure. Standard engineering requires FoS ≥ 1.5 to 2.0.
