Welcome to Structura

Build 2D structures and watch them react to gravity, wind, rain, and applied forces. Stress is computed from how much each beam stretches or compresses under load.

  • Node tool: click to place a joint. N
  • Beam tool: click a node, drag to another, release to connect. B
  • Pin tool: click a node to anchor it in place (triangle support). P
  • Force tool: click a node, drag to set direction/magnitude. F
  • Delete: click any element to remove it. X
  • Select: click to inspect + tweak properties. S
  • Pan: middle-click drag, or hold Space+drag. Scroll = zoom.
  • Play toggles physics. Reset returns to the saved layout.

Try a preset from the toolbar to see a pre-designed structure, then adjust sliders on the right to load it with wind, rain, or extreme gravity.
