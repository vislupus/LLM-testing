FORGE
Interactive structural simulation

Design structures, then push them with weight, wind, rain, flood, heat, pressure, and motion. Color is live stress. Red is a warning. White-hot is failure.

Build

  • Place nodes, then connect them with beams.
  • Anchors never move. Rollers hold vertically.
  • Pick a material — steel, cable, rubber, carbon…
  • Weights hang extra mass from a joint.

Simulate

  • Run to apply gravity and environment.
  • Drag a joint while running to pull on it.
  • Vehicles drive across and load the deck.
  • Reset restores the original geometry.

Read the structure

  • Cyan/green = comfortable. Amber = working hard.
  • Red = over capacity. Member may snap.
  • Cables carry tension only. Concrete hates tension.
  • Heat expands members and can lock in stress.

Forces

  • Wind grows with height and gusts.
  • Rain adds weight; flood adds buoyancy & drag.
  • Quake shakes every anchor.
  • Pressure is a uniform downward live load.
