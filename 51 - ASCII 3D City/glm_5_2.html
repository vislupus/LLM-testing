THE CITY

Thirteen by thirteen blocks of rain and light. Six districts, one orbital rail, five glyph shards scattered through the memory of the machine that dreams this place. Signs change their minds. Traffic obeys the lights. Inhabitants walk the awnings. The weather is not decoration — it is a mood, and it can be bargained with at the Rain Shrine in Ashline Works.

Nothing here is drawn with pictures. Every shape you see is a character: the buildings are @, the rain is /, the inhabitants are o. Move fast and the glyphs smear.
