How to survive the glyph‑rain
This city is procedural and systemic: follow traffic, listen for trains, watch for sign glitches. The world hides rooftop routes, maintenance tunnels, and an impossible district that only appears when you know where to look.
Exploration
  • Click to lock pointer; Esc to release.
  • Shift sprint drains stamina; stop to recover.
  • Fog + rain change readability: get closer to read signs.
Secrets
  • Find terminal shrines across districts to unlock a hidden transit.
  • Watch for a sign that spells your coordinates incorrectly.
  • Some alleys only exist during certain weather.
