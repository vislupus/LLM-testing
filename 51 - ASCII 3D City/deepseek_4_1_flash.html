FIELD MANUAL//

    

      

        HOW THE CITY WORKS

        ▸ Buildings, streets and altitude are a live voxel field. Glyphs are chosen by light: dark surfaces get sparse marks, lit ones get heavy ink.

        ▸ Windows are lit by floor-light lattices inside each structure. Blackout events kill them.

        ▸ Rain is unrendered data. When the streets are wet they smear neon back at you.

        ▸ Headlights, taillights and sign colours are the only true light in the gutters.
      

      

        THINGS TO DO

        ▸ Stand in the middle of a road and let the traffic bump you. They will honk.

        ▸ Take a lift podium to a rooftop, then glide between towers.

        ▸ Find the 8 fragments (glowing ◆ shards on high places and under things).

        ▸ Find 6 monolith stones hiding in alleys, decks and islands. They spell a word.

        ▸ Open the Spire door when the word is whole.

        ESC closes this. Text is the whole universe here — read it.
