Theory Reference

Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution

f(v) = 4πv² (m/2πkT)^(3/2) e^(-mv²/2kT)

In 2D, the distribution becomes:

P(v) ∝ v · e^(-mv²/2kT)

Ideal Gas Law (2D)

PV = NkT

For area A: P = NkT/A
