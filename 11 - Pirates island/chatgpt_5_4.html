How to Play
Drag with the mouse to orbit the camera and use the wheel to zoom.

1–3 select a ship.
W/S or ↑/↓ move forward/back.
A/D or ←/→ turn.
Space fires the selected ship at the nearest enemy in range.

Between waves you can spend gold on damage, fire rate, and movement speed for the selected ship.
