XV-DAWN

You are standing on a quiet, unfamiliar world: crystal plains, fungal groves, basalt ridges, ancient ruins, drifting wildlife, and breathing cave mouths.

Controls (typical VR controllers):
• Left stick: move
• Right stick X: turn
• Trigger near an artifact: grab/release
• Bring a grabbed artifact close to your headset: analyze/collect
• Press a face button (A/X or similar): Scan pulse (reveals points of interest)

WebXR required. No non‑XR fallback is provided.
