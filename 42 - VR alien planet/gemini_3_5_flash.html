Welcome, Pioneer. You have touched down on the uncharted surface of Kronos IV.

YOUR MISSION: Explore the alien terrain, descend into the deep crystalline caves, find the precursor ruins, and locate 5 active anomalies (Artifacts).

CONTROLS (VR):
• Left thumbstick: Glide smoothly across the planet surface and caves.
• Right thumbstick: Turn smoothly.
• Right Controller Laser: Point at floating artifacts.
• Right Controller Trigger: Hold down to cast a collection beam.

CONTROLS (DESKTOP):
• Mouse drag: Look around.
• W/A/S/D: Move.
• Left-Click & Hold: Target and scan artifacts with center-crosshair.
