AETHELGARD SIMULATION GUIDE

🌍 Procedural Autonomous Living World

Aethelgard simulates an autonomous world. Agents make independent choices based on hunger, wealth, genetics, health, and occupations. They forage, farm fertile lands, mine mountains for ore, build cities, establish trade routes, wage wars, research technology, pass down genetic traits to offspring, and perish from famine, old age, or plague.

🧬 Evolving Genetics & Natural Selection

Every agent has an individual genome: Strength, Intelligence, Curiosity, Aggression, Immunity, Fertility, Industriousness, and Charisma. Natural selection operates continuously: epidemics weed out vulnerable agents, wars cull the weak, and innovative minds advance their civilization into new scientific eras.

🏛 Cities & Dynamic Trade Routes

Cities sprout naturally near valuable resources (wheat fields, deep forests, iron veins, coastal fish). Cities develop roads and sea routes to trade surplus goods. Active trade boosts prosperity and tech exchange, but can also carry virulent pathogens.

⚡ Disasters, God Powers & Foreign Voyagers

Use the God Powers panel to unleash droughts, locust swarms, extreme winter blizzards, floods, boredom, or war fever. You can also inject Foreign Agents with distinct genetics and advanced capabilities to see how native civilisations react!
