Interphase Cell State

You are observing a metabolic runtime environment of a homeostatic somatic eukaryotic cell. Thousands of active elements map real protein synthesis pathways:

Ribosomes parse mRNA strands to synthesize raw polypeptides, passing them directly into the lumina of the Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER).

Molecules are packed into Vesicles, migrating systematically toward the Golgi Apparatus for glycan post-translational structural modification, sort tagging, and terminal destination packaging.

Mitochondria leverage electrochemical proton gradients across cristae barriers to build ATP molecular fuel, powering structural motor steps along structural Microtubules.
