    About this simulation

    This is a real-time WebGPU visualization of a simplified animal cell. Geometry, lighting, and motion are generated on the GPU; nothing is loaded from external 3D assets.

    Camera: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

    Not to scale. Ribosomes, pores, and particles are enlarged so pathways can be seen. A typical animal cell is 10–30 µm across; a ribosome is ~25 nm.

    Biological simplifications: 8 representative chromosomes (humans have 46), continuous secretory traffic, compressed timescales, and no molecular collision chemistry.

    Limitation: transparent organelles are not order-independent; overlapping membranes can show blending artifacts. Mitosis shows mechanics, not a full molecular checkpoint model.

    WebGPU is required

    This educational simulation uses the WebGPU API. Please open it in a current version of Chrome, Edge, or another browser with WebGPU enabled.
