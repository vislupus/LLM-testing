The eukaryotic cell
A membrane-bound compartment where DNA → RNA → protein flows through an endomembrane assembly line. Click any structure for its function, or switch to Mitosis to watch chromosome condensation, spindle formation, alignment, separation and cytokinesis. Follow the glowing particles: mRNA leaves the nucleus, ribosomes translate it, the ER folds the protein, the Golgi sorts it, and vesicles secrete it at the membrane.
