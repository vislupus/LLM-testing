About
A tesseract is the 4D analog of a cube — 8 cubic cells, 24 faces, 32 edges, 16 vertices.

Inner cube (smaller, dimmer) and outer cube (larger, brighter) are connected by edges through the 4th dimension.

Rotation occurs in 2D planes within 4D space (XY, XZ, XW, YZ, YW, ZW).
