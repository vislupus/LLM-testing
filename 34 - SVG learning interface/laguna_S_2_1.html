Flower Parts

Flowers have four main parts: sepals (protect the bud), petals (attract pollinators), stamens (male reproductive organs), and pistils (female reproductive organs).

The receptacle is the swollen tip of the stem where flower parts attach. The stamen consists of a filament and an anther that produces pollen. The pistil has an ovary (contains ovules), a style, and a stigma (receives pollen).
